01 — Depth

I go deep into the actual problem

Most failed AI projects were framed wrong, not built wrong. I dig until I understand what’s really breaking — the model is usually the easy part once you know that.

02 — Honesty

I’ll say when AI isn’t the answer

Some problems need a SQL query and a cron job, not a model. I’d rather say that out loud than ship something impressive that quietly doesn’t work.

03 — Ownership

I ship it and then support it

Documentation, clean architecture and no black boxes. Whoever inherits my code should be able to read it, change it, and not need me on a call to do so.

04 — Measurable

Outcomes, not activity

Hours saved, errors removed, throughput gained. I like defining what success looks like before starting — and then actually measuring it afterwards.
